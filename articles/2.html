Paper Check: A Turnitin & iThenticate Primer论文查重：Turnitin 与 iThenticate 入门

June 14, 20262026年6月14日

Both Turnitin and iThenticate compare your document against enormous indexes — journals, web pages, and previously submitted student papers — and return a similarity percentage. That number alone tells you very little.

What counts as a match

A "match" is any stretch of text, usually a handful of consecutive words, that also appears somewhere in the index. Properly quoted material, your own bibliography, and even common phrases can all register as matches depending on how the report is configured.

Why 0% isn't the real goal

  • A correctly cited quote will always show as a match — that's expected and fine.
  • Most institutions care about unattributed matches, not the raw total.
  • A very low score on a heavily-cited literature review can actually be a red flag that sources aren't being referenced at all.

Reading the report properly

Look at the source breakdown, not just the headline number. A handful of long matches to a single source is a different problem than dozens of short matches scattered across many sources — the second is usually just common academic phrasing.

Turnitin 和 iThenticate 都会将你的文档与庞大的数据库进行比对——期刊、网页内容，以及以往学生提交过的论文——然后返回一个相似度百分比。但这个数字本身能说明的信息其实很有限。

什么算作“匹配”

所谓“匹配”，是指一段文字（通常是连续的几个词）与数据库中的某处内容相同。正确引用的内容、你自己的参考文献列表，甚至一些常见表达，根据报告的设置不同，都可能被记为匹配。

为什么0%并不是真正的目标

  • 正确标注来源的引文始终会显示为匹配——这是正常且没有问题的。
  • 大多数院校关心的是未标注来源的匹配内容，而不是原始总匹配率。
  • 在引用文献密集的文献综述中，相似度过低反而可能是一个警示信号，说明来源根本没有被引用。

正确解读报告

要关注来源明细，而不仅仅是首页的总体数字。与单一来源出现几处长段匹配，和与众多来源出现几十处零散短匹配，是完全不同性质的问题——后者通常只是常见的学术表达方式。
