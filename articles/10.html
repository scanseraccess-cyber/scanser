Student Review: Cutting Revision Time in Half

July 12, 2026

A final-year student shared how they restructured their pre-submission routine around checking similarity and AI-detection together, rather than one after the other at the last minute.

The old routine

Write the full draft, run a similarity check a day before the deadline, panic-edit whatever gets flagged, and hope nothing else comes up.

What changed

  • Running both reports after each major section was finished, not just at the end — catching issues while the context was still fresh.
  • Using the AI report's sentence highlighting to identify which paragraphs needed a real rewrite versus a light touch-up.
  • Keeping a simple before/after log so nothing had to be re-checked twice.

The result

Spreading the checks across the writing process, instead of saving them for the end, meant no more all-nighters spent rewriting an entire chapter the night before a deadline.
