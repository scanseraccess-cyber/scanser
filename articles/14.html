Model Ranking: How We Compare AI Writing Models

July 25, 2026

The Model Ranking page summarizes, by model family, the average AI-detection rate and similarity rate observed after a standard rewrite pass.

What "detection rate" means here

The average probability score a mainstream AI detector assigned to a rewritten sample from that model family, across a broad set of test passages.

What "similarity rate" means here

The average match percentage returned by a standard plagiarism check on the same rewritten samples, reflecting how much of the output still overlaps with commonly indexed sources.

Why the numbers move over time

  • Both detectors and underlying models are updated regularly, so relative rankings shift.
  • Results vary by subject area — technical writing and narrative writing don't behave identically.
  • Lower is better on both metrics; neither number alone tells the whole story, which is why both are shown side by side.
