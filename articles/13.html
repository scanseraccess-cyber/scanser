Building a Rewrite History You Can Trust建立一份可信赖的改写记录

July 21, 20262026年7月21日

Keeping a record of what changed between drafts is useful for more than just your own reference.

For your advisor

Being able to show exactly which passages were reworded, and why, makes any conversation about your writing process faster and more transparent.

For a committee or reviewer

If a similarity or AI score is ever questioned, having a dated history of your revisions is far more convincing than trying to reconstruct the timeline from memory afterward.

For your own peace of mind

  • You can always roll back a rewrite that changed the meaning more than you intended.
  • You have a clear before/after comparison if a supervisor asks what specifically was reworded.
  • It removes the anxiety of wondering whether you've "lost" an earlier, better version of a paragraph.

Your Rewrite History page keeps this automatically — it's worth glancing back through before a final submission.

记录每次草稿之间的改动，其用处远不止是方便你自己查阅。

对导师而言

能够准确说明哪些段落被改写过、为什么改写，会让任何关于你写作过程的沟通更高效、更透明。

对答辩委员会或评审而言

如果查重或AI分数受到质疑，一份带有时间记录的修改历史，远比事后凭记忆重新拼凑时间线更有说服力。

对你自己而言，也更安心

  • 如果某次改写偏离原意超出预期，你随时可以回退。
  • 如果导师询问具体改写了哪些内容，你可以拿出清晰的修改前后对比。
  • 不用再担心自己是否“弄丢”了某段更好的早期版本，减少不必要的焦虑。

你的改写记录页面会自动保存这些内容——最终提交前，值得回头翻看一遍。
