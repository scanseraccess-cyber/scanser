Exporting and Sharing Your Reports Properly

July 29, 2026

How you share a report matters almost as much as what's in it.

PDF vs. link sharing

A PDF export is a fixed snapshot — useful when you need to prove exactly what a report showed on a given date. A shared link can update live, which is convenient for ongoing collaboration but less reliable as a permanent record.

What advisors actually need to see

  • The overall score, but also the source/sentence breakdown — not just the headline number.
  • The date the check was run, especially if your document has changed since.
  • Confirmation of which exclusions (quotes, bibliography, thresholds) were applied.

Keeping a clean paper trail

Save exported reports with a consistent naming pattern — document name plus date — so that if a similarity or AI score is ever questioned months later, you can immediately produce the exact report that was generated at submission time.
