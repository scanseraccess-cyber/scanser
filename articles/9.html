Common Mistakes Before Submitting a Thesis提交论文前常见的错误

July 9, 20262026年7月9日

Most last-minute thesis problems aren't about the writing itself — they're about process.

Formatting slips

Margins, heading styles, and page numbering that drift from the required template are among the most common reasons a submission gets bounced back for revision.

Citation gaps

An in-text citation with no matching bibliography entry (or vice versa) is easy to miss on your own and easy for a reader to catch.

Version-control mixups

  • Submitting a draft that's missing your advisor's most recent comments.
  • Two people editing the same file separately and losing each other's changes.
  • Running your plagiarism check on one version and submitting a different one.

The fix

Run your final checks — similarity, AI report, formatting — on the exact file you intend to submit, as the very last step, not on an earlier draft.

大多数论文提交前的临时问题，其实并不出在写作本身，而是出在流程上。

格式失误

页边距、标题样式、页码编号偏离规定模板，是论文被退回要求修改的最常见原因之一。

引用缺失

正文中的引用在参考文献列表中找不到对应条目（或反过来），这种问题自己很容易忽略，但读者却很容易发现。

版本混乱

  • 提交的草稿遗漏了导师最新的修改意见。
  • 两人分别编辑同一份文件，导致彼此的修改互相覆盖丢失。
  • 查重检测用的是一个版本，实际提交的却是另一个版本。

解决办法

务必在最后一步，用你打算提交的那个确切文件，运行所有最终检查——查重、AI检测、格式核对——而不是用之前的某个草稿版本。
