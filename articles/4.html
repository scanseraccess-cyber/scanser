5 Habits That Quietly Raise Your AI Score5个悄悄拉高你AI分数的习惯

June 21, 20262026年6月21日

Some everyday writing habits happen to overlap with what detectors flag — even in text a person wrote entirely themselves.

1. Overusing em dashes

A stray em dash here and there is normal. A paragraph that leans on it in almost every sentence starts to look templated.

2. Uniform sentence length

If every sentence runs 18–22 words, vary it deliberately. Follow a longer sentence with a short one.

3. Starting every paragraph the same way

"Furthermore", "Additionally", "Moreover" stacked at the top of consecutive paragraphs is a pattern worth breaking up.

4. Over-hedging

Phrases like "it is important to note that" or "it could be argued that" repeated often can read as filler rather than argument.

5. Perfectly balanced lists

Three points, each exactly one sentence, in every section, is a rhythm worth interrupting occasionally — let some points run longer than others.

一些日常写作习惯，恰好与检测工具所标记的特征重合——即便这段文字完全是本人亲笔所写。

1. 过度使用破折号

偶尔用一两个破折号很正常。但如果一段话里几乎每句都用破折号，就会显得像是套模板写出来的。

2. 句长过于一致

如果每句话都是差不多的长度，就要有意识地做出变化，比如在长句之后接一个短句。

3. 每段开头都一样

“此外”“另外”“而且”这类词连续出现在每一段的开头，是一种值得打破的模式。

4. 过度使用模糊限定

“值得注意的是”“可以认为”这类表达如果反复使用，读起来会像是凑字数，而不是真正的论证。

5. 过于工整的列表

每个部分都恰好三点、每点都正好一句话，这种节奏值得偶尔打破——让某些要点写得比其他的更长一些。
