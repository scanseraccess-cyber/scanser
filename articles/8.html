Reading a Plagiarism Report

July 6, 2026

Like an AI report, a similarity report rewards a careful read over a glance at the top-line number.

Matched sources

The report lists every source with an overlapping passage, usually ranked by how much of your document matches each one. A single dominant source is a very different situation from many small matches spread thin.

Self-similarity

If you've published earlier work, drafts, or a thesis proposal that overlaps with your current paper, some databases will flag your own prior writing. This is normal and worth noting to your advisor rather than panicking over.

What most reports exclude by default

  • Properly quoted material (though this can usually be toggled on or off).
  • Your bibliography and reference list.
  • Small matches below a configurable word-count threshold.

Always check which exclusions are active — a report with quotes excluded will look very different from one that includes them.
