Reading an AI Detection Report

July 2, 2026

An AI detection report is more useful read section-by-section than as a single headline percentage.

The overall score

This is an estimated probability, not a verdict. Treat anything in a middle range (roughly 30–60%) as inconclusive rather than damning.

Sentence-level highlighting

Most reports color-code individual sentences or paragraphs by their contribution to the score. This is the most actionable part — it tells you exactly where to focus a rewrite, instead of reworking an entire document.

What counts as a "high-risk" paragraph

  • Unusually uniform sentence length and structure throughout.
  • Very low perplexity — safe, predictable word choices with little variation.
  • Repeated transition phrases or formulaic paragraph openings.

Fixing the two or three highest-scoring paragraphs usually moves the overall number more than a light edit across the whole document.
