Academic Tone Without Sounding Robotic

July 18, 2026

Formal writing doesn't have to mean stiff writing. A few small habits keep academic tone precise without draining the life out of it.

Vary your sentence openers

If several sentences in a row start with "This" or "The", rework at least one of them to start differently.

Use hedging sparingly and specifically

"It is possible that X may potentially contribute to Y" can usually be tightened to "X may contribute to Y" without losing appropriate caution.

Let some sentences breathe

  • Not every sentence needs a citation clause bolted onto the end.
  • Occasionally combine two related short sentences into one with a comma or semicolon.
  • Read a paragraph aloud — if you run out of breath, it's a sign to split it up.

Trust plain verbs

"Utilize" almost always just means "use." Precision comes from clear claims, not from inflated vocabulary.
